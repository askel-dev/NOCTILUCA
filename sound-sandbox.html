  • No Tone.Transport. Never started, never referenced.
  • No loops / sequences / tempo. Every sound traces to a slider move or a button press.
  • No LFOs. The shimmer in STRINGS is beating between detuned oscillators — detune is driven by order, so it slows to nothing as the flock aligns.

  • Attacks 200–800 ms. No transients anywhere, bells included.
